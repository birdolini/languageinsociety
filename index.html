Revision tips :) 📚

Take one topic at a time with your summary or handout. Read the explanation slowly, choose an example from class or everyday speech, and write how it shows the connection between language and social life. This helps you understand the concept as part of a real situation rather than memorising a definition on its own.

خذي كل مره موضوع واحد مع الملخص أو الهاند آوت، واقري الشرح على راحتك، وبعدها اختاري مثال من المحاضرة أو من الكلام اليومي واكتبي تحته كيف يبين العلاقة بين اللغة والحياة الاجتماعية. لما تراجعي بهالطريقة، بتصير الفكرة أوضح عندكِ لأنكِ ربطتي التعريف بسياقه ومثاله، بدل ما تحفظيه بروحه من غير ما تعرفي وين وكيف يُستخدم.

  1. 1

    Begin with the main question 📌

    Read the heading and ask what relationship between language and society this topic wants you to examine.

  2. 2

    Explain the key terms 🖍️

    Write a short meaning beside each term in your own words, then find where it appears in an example.

  3. 3

    Connect the idea to an example 🔎

    Use an example from class, a handout, or everyday speech to show how the concept works in context.

  4. 4

    Check what the evidence shows ✅

    Before writing a conclusion, ask what evidence supports your point and what would require more observation.
