# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript, confirmed in `AGENTS.md`; deploy to GitHub Pages or an equivalent static host.

## Users

ENGL3328 Language in Society students who need a clear, low-friction way to orient themselves in the course and review its concepts between classes.

## Product Purpose

An independent study companion that makes the course's connected themes approachable and prepares students for later revision and research-project support.

## Positioning

A lightweight, locally grounded companion to private course materials: it organizes original study guidance around Omani/Arabic sociolinguistic contexts without replacing the official course portal or publishing readings.

## Operating Context

Students use it on an iPad first (768–1024px, portrait or landscape), with phone and desktop access also supported. Course handouts are the authoritative private source material.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The first release includes a static homepage and one Foundations topic page. It has no additional lessons, quiz, accounts, submission workflow, data collection, tracking, backend, or external dependencies. It must be accessible, keyboard usable, fast, and deploy from the repository root.

## Brand Commitments

The user requested a neat, clean, cute study site. The typographic site identity is “Sociolinguistics for Habi 👩🏻🦜”, using Apple-style emoji assets. Student-facing copy is short, direct, warm, and academically accurate; avoid promotional language and generic AI-style phrasing.

## Evidence on Hand

Course outline, project assignment/submission instructions, activity handouts, readings, and a project analysis workbook live in `handouts_readonly/`. They may inform original summaries but must not be published as site assets. No real testimonials, participant data, research findings, or public course assets are available.

## Product Principles

- Make the course feel navigable before making it exhaustive.
- Keep sociolinguistic concepts accurate and locally grounded.
- Treat study support as calm guidance, not gamified productivity pressure.
- Let the homepage be useful without implying unavailable features.
- Preserve student privacy and academic integrity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic structure, readable contrast, visible focus, touch-friendly controls, reduced-motion support, and responsive reflow. The primary composition serves iPad portrait and landscape use.
