# Lesson authoring notes

Use this when writing or editing student-facing lesson material.

## English
- Write complete, clear explanations before adding decorative copy.
- Explain a concept, then show what a student should notice in an example.
- Avoid promotional language, generic encouragement, choppy fragments, and vague “study” phrasing.
- Write like a teacher's notes, not a description advertising the page. Name the subject directly; avoid lines such as “explained through a small field notebook, with English and Omani Arabic explanations.” This applies to headings, introductions, and metadata too.
- Do not present variation as an error or rank one form as inherently better than another.

## Arabic and Omani Arabic
- Write Arabic as natural Arabic, not as a word-for-word translation of the English.
- Use **Playpen Sans Arabic** for Arabic content and set `lang="ar" dir="rtl"`.
- When directly addressing the student, use the user-approved Omani feminine forms:
  - `تراجعي`, not `تراجعين`
  - `تكتبي`, `تلاحظي`, `تقدري`, `تشوفي`, `تعيشي`, `تدرسي`
- Use user-approved plural forms where relevant:
  - `يبينوا`, not `يبينون`
  - `يأثروا`, not `يأثرون`
  - `يقوّوا`, not `يقوّون`
- Prefer `ما قاعدة` rather than `مو قاعدة` in the lesson’s Omani Arabic.
- For this lesson’s explanatory negative phrasing, use forms such as `ما دليل إنه شي غلط` or `ما يعني إنه شي غلط`, not `مب دليل إن في غلط`.
- The approved Omani Arabic example is `ما سويت`; do not replace it with generic Gulf examples. Explain it as a page-specific example, not as a form used by every person in Oman.
- Keep Arabic explanations full and readable. Explain the idea in context instead of adding only a short Arabic label below English text.

## Bilingual lesson pattern
1. Explain the idea in clear English.
2. Give a natural Arabic explanation that makes the idea easier to understand.
3. Use a clearly framed example.
4. State what a student should observe before making a claim.
5. Preserve uncertainty: never generalise from one example or one speaker.

## Emoji and visual details
- Use Apple-style emoji images through `assets/apple-emoji.js` or the Emoji CDN, not native system emoji.
- Keep decorative emoji `aria-hidden`.
